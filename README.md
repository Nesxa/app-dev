# My Favorite Movie

## Title: *Maleficent*

**Genre:** Fantasy, Adventure  
**Release Year:** 2014  

---

## Why I Like It
Maleficent is my favorite movie because it shows a different side of a character who is usually seen as a villain. Instead of simply being evil, the movie explains that Maleficent became cold and angry because of betrayal and deep pain. This made me realize that people are not purely good or bad—many are shaped by what they have experienced.

I also like the movie because of the strong emotional story between Maleficent and Aurora. At first, Maleficent curses Aurora out of anger, but as time passes, she begins to care for her. Their bond slowly grows into something genuine, showing that love can appear in unexpected ways.

The film is also meaningful because it teaches forgiveness and healing. It shows that even after great hurt, a person can still change and choose kindness instead of revenge.

## Main Characters
Maleficent – A powerful fairy who becomes dark after betrayal but later learns to love and forgive.
Aurora – A kind princess who brings light and change into Maleficent’s life.
King Stefan – A greedy and ambitious ruler whose choices lead to betrayal and conflict.
